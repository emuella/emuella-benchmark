# Classic parallel execution: finite rejection

The selected 4W policy **failed the frozen promotion gate**. All 1,920 RarePlanes
confirmation calls were exact, all eighteen one-worker encode contrasts were
equivalent, and every encode upper change bound was below +5%. Nevertheless,
neither Boca Raton PAN16 nor MS16 reached the required improved classification
in either style. Their intervals are inconclusive against the 5% improvement
gate, not evidence of regression. No source retuning, sample replacement,
outlier removal or threshold change followed confirmation. The production writer
retains its original W joined batches and unchanged working requirements.

The [frozen protocol](classic-parallel-execution.md) and
[complete factual record](evidence/classic-parallel-execution.json) retain all
case means, 99% relative intervals, byte counts and separate resource/scaling
observations. The separately required 480-call SpaceNet and 1,440-call matched
OpenJPEG encode cohorts completed after rejection to preserve full coverage;
they could not rescue the failed gate. All protected streams, raw per-process
receipts and original notices remain in their approved stores. These are
previously measured products on one host, with per-contrast confidence only.
